# Code of Conduct

CoStudio welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels.
This policy applies in the project’s issues, reviews, discussions, and other
spaces where people represent the project.

- Be patient and specific. Critique a decision, behavior, or change without
  attacking the person who made it.
- Respect people’s identities, names, boundaries, and privacy.
- Do not harass, threaten, stalk, publish private information, use slurs, or
  target people with unwanted attention.
- Avoid posting personal images, backups, logs, or other private data without
  permission.

Report private matters through a repository maintainer or GitHub’s reporting
tools. Do not post sensitive reports in public issues. Maintainers should
respect confidentiality, hear affected people, and document their decisions
privately. Responses may include a warning, removing material, restricting
participation, or referring a report to GitHub. Maintainers involved in a
report should recuse themselves.
